# What to verify on campus (survey checklist)
Method: Map tab -> Edit mode -> Satellite layer -> drag each marker onto its building, or stand at the spot and press "Set to my GPS position" (accuracy under ~15 m outdoors). Then press "Export corrected data".
1. Real name and block number of every building (academic blocks 1-12, IET, IBM, Pharmacy, Applied Sciences, Education).
2. Position of: main gate, other gates, admin block, central library, auditorium, seminar halls, all ~4 canteens, food court, store.
3. Each of the 12 boys and 4 girls hostel blocks (add one marker each in data.js).
4. Sports grounds, gym, medical centre/dispensary, bank/ATM, parking areas.
5. Phone numbers: campus security, medical centre. Timings: library, admin office, dispensary.
6. Official GLA logo and brand colours (ask the university's media/IT cell).
Sources used: Wikipedia, Wikimapia, Collegedunia, Zollege, Shiksha, Careers360, Edumyra. Third-party sites can be outdated; confirm with gla.ac.in or staff.
